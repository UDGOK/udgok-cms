How it works

From QR to accountability

Step 1

Create a tool

Add name and details. toolQR generates a unique QR instantly.

Step 2

Print a label

Use regular paper or weather-proof stock. Batch print for many tools.

Step 3

Stick it on

Apply to the tool (and case/charger if needed). You’re live.

Step 4

Scan to check-out/in

Crew scans with any phone to log who has it - fast and accountable.
