Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my tool inventory?

Tracking your tool inventory is simple with ShareMyToolbox. Our user-friendly app allows you to add each tool to your inventory and assign it to a specific user or location. You can update the tool’s status, such as “Loaned” or “in repair,” and even set reminders for maintenance schedules. With real-time updates and easy reporting, you’ll always know where your tools are and who is responsible for them.

Can you put a tracker on tools?

Tracking tools with bluetooth is usually not reliable. Tracking all your small tools with GPS is usually not affordable or feasible. That’s where ShareMyToolbox comes in. Instead, our methodology is to have people take ownership of tools, building accountability within the team. We keep it simple. Still want to have more? No problem. We allow the option to turn on GPS tracking each time an asset tag is scanned. Utilizing the phone’s GPS info, we help you track exactly where the tool was when it was scanned.

What does tracking tools do?

Tracking tools with ShareMyToolbox provides numerous benefits for your company. It allows you to monitor the location and usage of your tools, reducing the risk of misplacement or theft. You can optimize tool allocation, streamline maintenance schedules, and improve overall tool management. With our tool tracking system, you’ll enhance productivity, save costs, and ensure that your tools are being utilized efficiently.

How do I keep track of jobsite tools?

Keeping track of jobsite tools is easy with ShareMyToolbox. Our app enables you to assign specific tools to different jobsites or users, making it clear where each tool should be located. You can also set up job-specific kits and track tool movements between various jobsites. With real-time updates and notifications, managing your jobsite tools becomes a seamless process.

Can Milwaukee and Dewalt tools be tracked?

Yes, you can track Milwaukee and Dewalt tools, along with tools from other manufacturers, using ShareMyToolbox. Our tool tracking system is compatible with all tool brands, making it suitable for construction companies and contractors with diverse tool inventories. Simply add the tool details to your inventory, and you can easily track, manage, and maintain all your tools, regardless of the brand.

How do construction companies track equipment?

Construction companies can efficiently track equipment with ShareMyToolbox’s equipment tracking solution. Our app enables you to assign equipment to specific projects, users, or job sites, keeping track of usage and maintenance schedules. You can also set up alerts for equipment servicing or replacements, ensuring that your equipment is always in optimal condition. With our comprehensive equipment tracking system, construction companies can enhance equipment management and improve project efficiency.

Is there a mobile app for tool tracking?

Yes! ShareMyToolbox offers a mobile app for iOS and Android, allowing teams to track, assign, and transfer tools directly from their smartphones. Field workers can check tools in and out, take photos, and log tool conditions—all from the jobsite. The app ensures real-time updates, so the office and field teams always have access to the latest tool inventory.

Can I generate reports on tool usage and inventory?

Yes! ShareMyToolbox includes reporting features that allow users to track:
Complete Item History and Usage
Audits and Audit History
Costing History
These reports provide valuable insights for improving efficiency, reducing losses, and planning tool purchases.

What are the benefits of tool tracking software over spreadsheets?

While spreadsheets can store tool data, they require manual updates, are prone to errors, and lack real-time tracking. ShareMyToolbox offers several advantages:
✅ Current updates – See where tools are and who last scanned them
✅ Mobile access – Workers can check tools in/out from the field
✅ Accountability – Digital history of tool transfers reduces loss
✅ Alerts & reminders – Get notified when tools are overdue
✅ Maintenance tracking – Log tool conditions and repairs easily
✅ Scalability – Manage thousands of tools without complex spreadsheets

Does ShareMyToolbox help with OSHA compliance and safety?

Yes! ShareMyToolbox supports OSHA compliance by ensuring tools are properly maintained and accounted for. Users can log inspection dates, maintenance records, and tool conditions to prevent unsafe equipment from being used. With a clear record of tool health and availability, companies can reduce workplace accidents and meet safety requirements more easily.

How can tool tracking improve jobsite efficiency?

Tool tracking reduces wasted time searching for tools and ensures the right equipment is always available when needed. ShareMyToolbox improves efficiency by:
Reducing tool loss – Fewer misplaced or stolen tools
Speeding up tool transfers – Employees can easily find and request tools
Minimizing downtime – Ensuring tools are maintained and ready to use
Improving job costing – Tracking tool usage per project for better financial insights
With a streamlined tracking system, workers can focus on getting the job done instead of hunting for tools.

Can I track who last used a tool?

Absolutely! ShareMyToolbox keeps a complete history of tool assignments, showing who checked out a tool and when. This improves accountability and helps teams quickly track down missing or overdue equipment. By maintaining a clear chain of custody, businesses can reduce loss, encourage responsibility, and avoid unnecessary tool replacements. With optional GPS location tagging when an Item is scanned, you’ll know exactly where the tool was the last time it was scanned.
