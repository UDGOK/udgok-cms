## Bug found and fixed during QA

**Severity:** HIGH — vendor PO response links from buyer emails were
unreachable.

**Reproduction:**
1. Buyer issues a PO → portal link in email is `https://cms.udgok.com/p/<token>`
2. Vendor clicks the link
3. Gets redirected to `/sign-in?redirect_url=/p/...` ❌
4. Vendor has no Clerk account → stuck

**Root cause:** `middleware.ts` `isPublicRoute` had the RFQ portal
(`/q/(.*)`, `/api/q/(.*)`) but not the PO portal (`/p/(.*)`,
`/api/p/(.*)`). Clerk's middleware matched `/p/*` as a protected
route and 307-redirected to sign-in.

**Fix:** Added two lines to `isPublicRoute`:
```diff
  '/q/(.*)',
  '/api/q/(.*)',
+ '/p/(.*)',
+ '/api/p/(.*)',
```

**Verification after deploy** (buildId `dpl_Fidy1d8nvtRejJ2ohHmSjoezwaWa`):
- `GET /p/bogus` → 200 (renders 410 expired view) ✓
- `POST /api/p/bogus` → 404 (no such token, route exists) ✓
- `GET /q/bogus` → 200 (no regression) ✓

**Commit:** `0b21e7b fix: vendor PO portal /p/[token] and /api/p/[token] were auth-gated`

**Also cleaned up:** an empty `app/api/cron/rfq-reminders/`
directory that was shadowing the route. Real cron routes are
`/api/cron/rfq-expire` and `/api/cron/rate-limit-gc` — both
return 401 without `Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET`.
